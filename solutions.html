The details

Good questions.
Real answers.

Can Emerald help with a completely custom package?+

Yes. Our team can support concept development, film structure, printing, format selection, and converting decisions based on your product and equipment.

Do you offer packaging for fresh-cut produce?+

Yes. We offer structures and formats designed around breathability, anti-fog, oxygen transmission, and pack-line needs for fresh-cut and ready-to-eat produce.

Are recycled-content materials available?+

Selected food-contact packaging solutions can incorporate up to 30% post-consumer recycled content. Actual suitability depends on your application and specifications.

Can your team advise on sustainability claims?+

We can help explore material choices and relevant standards. Final claims and labeling should be verified for the exact product, market, and available infrastructure.
