The Emerald story

From a single press
to a new chapter.

1963

It starts in Berkeley.

James Kelly and three partners open a small California operation with one four-color press and two bag machines, making bags for neighborhood bakeries.

01
1979

Fresh thinking takes hold.

An opportunity in the Salinas Valley changes everything. Specialized lettuce packaging points Emerald toward a future in fresh produce.

02
1993

The family takes the lead.

James Kelly acquires his partners' interests and the business moves to a larger Union City facility, ready for its next era.

03
Today

Generations of know-how.

Under CEO Kevin Kelly and president Pallavi Joyappa, Emerald combines family ownership with experienced professional leadership.

04
2026

Room to grow.

The acquisition of flexible packaging manufacturing assets in Santa Ana establishes a second California operation and supports customers across the Southwest and northern Mexico.
